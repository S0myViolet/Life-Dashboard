Pair with your dashboard

In the dashboard, open Settings → Chrome helper and create a pairing code. It is valid for 10 minutes and works once. Chrome will ask to let the helper reach your dashboard's address; that is the only site besides chatgpt.com and claude.ai it can talk to.

What it collects

Only conversations you selected on the dashboard, and only the user and assistant text Chrome rendered while the conversation was open in a tab. Long chats are drawn a window at a time, so older messages are collected when you scroll through them once. It never reads cookies or passwords, never calls ChatGPT's or Claude's internal APIs, and never collects attachments, images, hidden branches or anything that was not shown.

If a page is signed out, shows a verification check, or looks different than expected, the helper stops collecting that conversation and the dashboard shows Reconnect or Capture needs attention. Background revisits are an opt-in prototype in the popup; read its terms-of-use note before switching it on.
